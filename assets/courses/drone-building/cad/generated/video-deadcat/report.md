# Video kit — deadcat CF stand-in (props out of the camera view)

Variant `video-deadcat` · kit **video** · generated by `scripts/build_drone_model.py` from `cad/bom.json` (parts list v2) + `cad/variants.json`. 1 mm voxel model — a layout stand-in for fit, mass, and physics before anything is printed or bought; not the final printable stock frame (that is the Onshape model, TODO).
