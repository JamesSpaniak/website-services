## V1 — "Can you read this?" (6 variants, 15–20 s, 9:16 + 4:5)

**Structure (same for all six; only the chart feature changes):**

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Aerial hook (gorge / mist / cliff snip) | **Can you read this?** | Music starts low |
| 2–7 s | Cut to the sectional crop; slow push-in to the feature; olive circle appears | The question (below) | — |
| 7–9 s | Hold on the circled feature | **Answer in 3… 2… 1…** | Soft tick |
| 9–15 s | Callout labels appear on the chart | The answer (below) | — |
| 15–18 s | Quick cut: the same figure inside a course lesson (screen recording) | **One of 600+ practice questions** **[verify count]** | — |
| 18–20 s | End card | **Try Unit 1 free · thedroneedge.com** | — |

Use crops from our own course figures (Jacksonville and the downloaded sectionals); put the real numbers from the crop in the answer text. Mark crops **"Training only — not for navigation."**

| Variant | Question | Answer (fill numbers from the crop) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `classb` | What do these two numbers mean? (Class B shelf, e.g. 70/40) | Class B airspace from **[4,000] ft MSL** up to **[7,000] ft MSL** here. Numbers are hundreds of feet above sea level. Part 107 needs ATC authorization (LAANC) to fly inside | Chart Users' Guide; 14 CFR 107.41 |
| `classd` | What does the number in brackets mean? (dashed blue Class D, e.g. [25]) | Class D airspace up to **[2,500] ft MSL**. Inside it, you need ATC authorization | Chart Users' Guide; 107.41 |
| `vignette` | Why is there a fuzzy magenta band here? | Inside the magenta shading, Class E airspace starts at **700 ft AGL** instead of 1,200 | Chart Users' Guide |
| `obstacle` | Which number is the tower's real height? | Bold **[1,749]** = top in feet MSL. **([1,049])** in parentheses = height above the ground | Chart Users' Guide |
| `restricted` | Can you fly in the blue hatched area? | **R-[XXXX]**: a restricted area. Check the chart margin for times and altitudes; when it's active, stay out | Chart Users' Guide; AIM 3-4 |
| `moa` | What is the magenta hatched area? | A **Military Operations Area (MOA)**: military training. Not prohibited, but look out and check if it's active | Chart Users' Guide; AIM 3-4 |

Headline (ad text): *"Most people can't read a sectional chart. The Part 107 test expects you to."* Primary text: *"Unit 1 of our Part 107 course is free. No card needed."* **[verify: no card needed for free preview]**

## V6 — Teacher testimonial (interview guide + edit)

**Before rolling:** signed release (template below) for the uses she chose; empty classroom; no students or student names in frame. Interviewer off camera and never in the edit. Ask her to **repeat the question in her answer**.

**Questions:**

1. Your name and what you teach. *(Only if attribution is approved.)*
2. Why did you want drones and Part 107 in your class?
3. What did the first few weeks look like?
4. Walk me through a class period with it.
5. How do your students react to it compared with other classwork?
6. What does the teacher dashboard let you see?
7. What surprised you?
8. What would you change or improve?
9. What would you tell another teacher thinking about it?
10. Describe it to a colleague in one sentence.
11. *(Audio-only backup)* Repeat answers 5, 9 and 10.

**Edit (60–90 s master):**

| Beat | Content |
|---|---|
| Open (0–8 s) | Her strongest line (usually Q5 or Q10) over B-roll of the empty classroom |
| Who (8–15 s) | Lower third: name/title or "CTE teacher, Pennsylvania" |
| Why (15–30 s) | Q2 |
| How (30–50 s) | Q4 + Q6 over dashboard (demo org) and Chromebook B-roll |
| Honest note (50–60 s) | A short piece of Q8 |
| Recommendation (60–75 s) | Q9 |
| End (75–90 s) | **Disclosure line**: *"Her school bought seats at a discounted pilot rate; she knew our team before the pilot."* + end card **thedroneedge.com/schools** |

Cutdowns: 30 s (open → Q5 → Q9 → disclosure → end), 15 s (one line + disclosure + end), 3 quote cards.
