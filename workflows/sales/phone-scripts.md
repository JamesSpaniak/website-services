# Phone scripts — school outreach

Phone supports email; it does not replace it. Default sequence: **email Day 0 → optional call Day 4–6** if no reply and a verified direct line exists.

Reference: [`outreach.md`](outreach.md), [`email-drafts.md`](email-drafts.md), [`docs/sales/rep-handoff.md`](../../docs/sales/rep-handoff.md).

---

## When to call

| Situation | Call? |
|-----------|-------|
| No email reply after 4–6 days; direct line on directory | Yes — reference email |
| Warm email reply; scheduling back-and-forth | Yes — book the meeting |
| Email bounced; phone is only verified channel | Yes — ask for correct email |
| Generic `info@` only | No — find named contact first |
| "Do not call" or district policy | No |

**Best times:** Tue–Thu, 9:00–11:30 or 1:30–3:30 local (avoid lunch and last period bell).

## Cold call — after email (recommended)

**Goal:** Book 15–30 minute discovery or confirm routing.

```text
Hi, is this [Name]?

[Name], this is [Your name] with Drone Edge — I sent a quick email about FAA Part 107 drone curriculum for [School/District]. I know inboxes are crowded; I wanted to see if that landed with the right person.

[PAUSE — let them respond]

If you're exploring drone or aviation pathways for students, we help schools run structured Part 107 prep in the browser with teacher progress dashboards — async or in class.

Would a 15-minute call this week or next be useful, or is there someone on the CTE or STEM side I should speak with instead?

[If yes → offer 2 times or send consultation link]
[If routing → get name, title, email; log in tracker]
[If not interested → thank them, update tracker to lost/nurture]
```

**If gatekeeper:**

```text
Hi, I'm trying to reach [Name] in [CTE / curriculum / STEM]. I'm [Your name] with Drone Edge — we support high school FAA Part 107 drone programs. Is [Name] available, or who handles drone or aviation curriculum decisions?
```

## Cold call — no prior email (use sparingly)

Only when fit is strong and you will send recap email immediately after.

```text
Hi, is this [Name]?

[Name], I'm [Your name] with Drone Edge. I'll be brief — I saw [personalization: CTE program, aviation pathway, STEM focus] at [School/District] and wanted to ask whether you're building a drone or Part 107 pathway for students.

We provide browser-based Part 107 prep with practice exams and a dashboard for teachers to track cohort progress.

Is that something you're looking at for [upcoming semester / next year], or should I connect with someone else on your team?

[Book call OR get referral OR permission to email overview]
```

Always follow with the standard first-touch email within 1 hour if they agree to email.

## Voicemail (under 25 seconds)

```text
Hi [Name], this is [Your name] with Drone Edge. I emailed about FAA Part 107 drone curriculum for [School/District]. If you're exploring drone programs for students, I'd welcome a quick call — my number is [number]. You can also book time at thedroneedge.com/consultation. Thanks.
```

Leave **one** voicemail per contact per campaign. Next touch = email follow-up.

## Warm callback — they replied to email

```text
Hi [Name], thanks for getting back to me — this is [Your name] from Drone Edge.

You mentioned [mirror their reply: timing, student count, CTE interest]. Before we pick a time, can I ask two quick things so the call is useful?

One — roughly how many students in year one?
Two — are you thinking Part 107 certification prep, a broader STEM pathway, or still exploring?

[Listen]

Great. Let's book [30 minutes / 15 minutes]. I can do [Option A] or [Option B] [timezone]. I'll send a calendar hold and links to our school overview.

Anything on procurement or grants I should know before we meet?
```

Capture before hanging up: student count, start date, tracks, decision maker, funding path.

## Discovery call — opening (first 2 minutes)

```text
Thanks for making time, [Name]. I have us down for [duration] to see if Drone Edge fits [School/District]'s drone or Part 107 plans.

Quick agenda: I'll ask a few questions about your program, show you how schools use the platform — especially the teacher dashboard — and we can decide if a pilot or quote makes sense. Sound good?

To start: what prompted the conversation, and what would success look like for your students this year or next?
```

Transition to qualification ([`rep-handoff.md`](../../docs/sales/rep-handoff.md) § Qualification).

## Discovery call — demo transitions

**School pages (no login):**

```text
I'll start with the public overview — this is what you'd share with a principal or grants office.
[Share /schools → /schools/curriculum → /schools/funding]
Part 107 is our live course today; creative and AI pathways are on the roadmap — I'll be straight about what's shipping now.
```

**Live product (student view):**

```text
This is what a student sees — structured units, sections, practice questions, unit exams. Progress saves as they go.
```

**Manager dashboard (critical):**

```text
This is usually what closes the loop for schools — your CTE lead or teacher sees every student, unit completion, and exam scores without chasing spreadsheets.
```

**Close the call:**

```text
Based on what you shared — [N] students, [timing], [Part 107 focus] — I'd recommend we look at [Pilot / Classroom] tier. I'll send a recap email today with links and next step: [quote / follow-up with grants office / nurture until fall].

Any concerns about privacy, student accounts, or PO process we should get ahead of?
```

## Objection snippets (phone)

| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| "Send me info" | "Absolutely — I'll email the school overview and curriculum page today. Is [email] best? And who else should I copy — CTE or grants?" |
| "We don't have budget" | "Understood. Many programs start with a small pilot semester or plan for the next grant cycle. Can I send funding-language notes — not eligibility promises — for your grants person?" |
| "We already have drones" | "Great — we're the knowledge and exam prep layer, not hardware. Part 107 content plus teacher visibility on top of what you fly." |
| "Is this an LMS?" | "Browser-based course platform with org accounts and a manager dashboard. SSO and Google Classroom are on the roadmap — what's blocking you today vs nice-to-have?" |
| "What's the price?" | "Depends on seat count and term — pilot semesters vs full-year classroom. I'll confirm numbers in a written quote within [X] days after we nail scope. Ballpark student count?" |

Full table: [`rep-handoff.md`](../../docs/sales/rep-handoff.md) § Objection handling.

## Compliance and professionalism

- Identify yourself and organization at start of every call.  
- Honor do-not-call / do-not-contact immediately.  
- Do not quote final B2B pricing on the phone unless leadership pre-approved bands.  
- Do not promise pass rates, grant approval, or ship dates for unreleased tracks.  
- Log call outcome same day: date, summary, next step, stage update.
