# Article statistics: drones, jobs, hobby, education, grants (Oct 2026)

The sourced numbers behind the three "gap" articles (see [`article-review-2026-10.md`](article-review-2026-10.md) §4), mapped to the claims in [`keynote-vision-two-gaps.md`](keynote-vision-two-gaps.md). Use this doc for the talk's Q&A prep too.

*Researched 2026-10-03 from web sources.*

**Confidence:**
- **H**: primary source (FAA, BLS, O*NET, ED, peer-reviewed paper), or several sources agree.
- **M**: secondary source, or not checked against the primary.
- **L**: vendor or anecdotal. Don't publish L numbers.

Re-check every regulatory date before publishing.

## 1. How the numbers support the keynote's claims

| Keynote claim | What the data says | Key stats |
|---|---|---|
| **Markets deliver drones as products** (claim 2) | Yes. Ownership and certificates are plentiful. | 837,513 drones registered; 1.23M TRUST certificates; ~481k–493k remote pilots |
| **The capability gap: certificates aren't the bottleneck** | Over a third of certificate holders don't fly commercially. The industry now talks about missing *specific expertise*, not missing pilots. | 36% of Part 107 holders flew recreationally only and 8% didn't fly (FAA survey, CY2024); ~1.16 remote pilots per active Part 107 aircraft |
| **Lateral spread only reaches people already in the hobby** (claim 3) | New hobbyist registrations are falling while the remaining hobbyists own more aircraft. The hobby is deepening, not widening. | New recreational registrations 6,053/mo (2023) → 4,782/mo (2025); aircraft per hobbyist 3.8 → 4.9 |
| **Hobbyists innovate and the industry grew out of them** (objection 3) | The firmware and the two best-known drone companies came from hobbyists, but most household innovation never spreads. | MultiWii → Baseflight → Cleanflight → Betaflight, all volunteer forks; DJI and 3DR founded by hobbyist user-entrepreneurs; only ~6% of household innovations diffuse |
| **The device isn't the bottleneck; the teacher is** (§2.7) | The strongest evidence is still OLPC Peru. Coaching research shows that investing in teachers moves outcomes. | OLPC: no gains after 10 years in 531 schools; coaching +0.18 SD achievement; under 40% of districts spend Title II-A on technology PD |
| **Schools reach everyone, but drone courses are rare** (§2.4) | CTE is huge, but drone courses appear in only a handful of state catalogs. | 8.6M high school CTE participants (2023-24); state UAS course codes confirmed in VA, TX, NC and TN |
| **Grants are low-durability funding** (§2.5) | Federal education funding was volatile in 2025–26, ESSER is gone, and federal money can no longer buy DJI or Autel. | Title IV-A held back for 25 days in Jul 2025; FY27 request folds 17 programs into one $2B grant; American Security Drone Act ban in effect since 2025-12-22 |
| **Hands-on building works** | Project-based learning has strong evidence. Drone-specific studies are small. | PBL meta-analysis d = 0.71; Knowledge in Action randomized trial +8/+10 points on AP pass rates |

**Hardware restrictions that affect grant purchases:**
- **American Security Drone Act**, in effect 2025-12-22: federal grant or contract funds can't buy or operate covered (DJI/Autel) drones. The universities that have published guidance apply it to research. **There is no ED guidance for K-12 Perkins or Title IV-A purchases**, so treat it as a real risk ([UNH guidance](https://www.unh.edu/research/research/compliance-safety/research-security/federal-prohibition-certain-drones/guidance), [OMB M-26-02](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/M-26-02-Ensuring-Government-Use-of-Secure-Unmanned-Aircraft-Systems-and-Supporting-United-States-Producers.pdf)). M-H.
- **FCC Covered List**, 2025-12-22: **all foreign-produced drones and critical components** (including flight controllers, radios, cameras, batteries and **motors**). No *new* equipment authorizations are issued; drones already authorized or owned are unaffected ([DA 25-1086](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-25-1086A1.pdf)). H.
  - Exemptions for Blue UAS and domestic end products were reported as running to 2027-01-01, then **extended to 2028-01-01 in July 2026**. The two research passes disagree; verify against FCC.
  - The FCC rule on logic-bearing components in new US-built drones takes effect **2026-10-13**.
  - DJI's Ninth Circuit challenge is pending; its opening brief is due 2026-11-02.
  - **Internal flag:** the Drone Building reference parts (Flywoo stack, XILO motors, RadioMaster XR2, BeeID) need checking against this before kits are quoted.
