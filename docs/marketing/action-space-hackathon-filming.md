## 5. Team interview (60–90 s per team)

Quiet corner, lav on the speaker, tripod, plain background. Each person says their **first name and team name** on camera. Ask them to repeat the question in the answer.

1. What are you building this weekend?
2. What's the hardest part?
3. What broke, and how did you fix it?
4. What should a high-school student learn first to do what you did this weekend?

Q4 ties the event to our audience. Keep the best answer from each team for the recap ending.
