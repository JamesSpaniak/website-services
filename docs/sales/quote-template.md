# Quote template — school packages

Copy into email or a PDF after filling brackets. Get leadership approval before sending **final** dollar amounts.

Link to [`packages.md`](packages.md) for tier definitions and checklist.

---

## Email cover (short)

```text
Subject: Drone Edge — quote for [Organization Name]

Hi [Name],

Thanks again for speaking with us about [program summary — e.g. CTE Part 107 pathway, Fall 2026].

Attached below is a draft quote for [Pilot / Classroom / Program] access to the Drone Edge platform. Summary:

- Organization: [Legal name]
- Term: [Start date] – [End date]
- Seats: [N] students
- Courses: FAA Part 107 — [course title]
- Manager accounts: [N]
- Total: $[AMOUNT] [USD] — [payment terms]

This quote is valid through [VALID THROUGH DATE].

Useful links:
- Schools overview: https://thedroneedge.com/schools
- Curriculum: https://thedroneedge.com/schools/curriculum
- Privacy: https://thedroneedge.com/privacy
- Terms: https://thedroneedge.com/legal

Next step: [PO / signed agreement / onboarding call date].

Best,
[Your name]
Drone Edge
```

## Quote body (structured)

```text
QUOTE — DRAFT
Quote #: [YYYY-MM-NNN]
Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
Valid through: [YYYY-MM-DD]

CUSTOMER
Organization: [District / School / College legal name]
Address: [Address]
Primary contact: [Name, title, email]
AP / billing contact: [If different]

OFFERING
Package: [Pilot | Classroom | Program / District]
Subscription term: [Start] – [End]

Included:
- Drone Edge platform access for up to [N] student seats
- Course: FAA Part 107 — [title] (Course ID [id] if known)
- [N] organization manager account(s) with progress dashboard
- Invite-code or bulk enrollment for students
- [Onboarding: 1× 60-min virtual session | as listed]

Not included:
- FAA knowledge test fees (paid by student/testing center)
- Drone hardware or field flight instruction
- Custom LMS integration (SSO/LTI not included unless separately agreed)
- Video & Photography / AI & Drones tracks (not yet generally available)

PRICING
Line item: [Package name] — [N] seats — $[AMOUNT]
[Optional line items]
Total: $[AMOUNT] USD

Payment terms: [Net 30 | Due on receipt | PO required: Yes/No]
Tax: [Exempt — attach certificate | Tax added if applicable]

IMPLEMENTATION (after acceptance)
- Launch target: [Date]
- Technical contact: [Email]
- Org setup by: Drone Edge operations (within [X] business days of PO/agreement)

LEGAL
Use of the platform is subject to:
- Terms of Service: https://thedroneedge.com/legal
- Privacy Notice: https://thedroneedge.com/privacy
[K-12 / student data exhibit if attached separately]

Accepted by:

Customer signature: _________________________  Date: _________
Print name / title:

Drone Edge signature: ______________________  Date: _________
```

## Appendix A stub (multi-year / district)

Use when selling **Program / District** with explicit renewal language:

```text
APPENDIX A — SUBSCRIPTION SUMMARY

Term: [1 | 2 | 3] year(s), beginning [date]
Auto-renewal: [Yes — 60-day notice | No — expires at term end]
Seat cap: [N] active students (hard cap | soft cap with true-up)
Courses licensed: [List]
Price year 1: $[AMOUNT]
[Price year 2/3 if fixed escalation]

Support: [Email | onboarding calls as listed]
```

Have legal review before first use with a district.
